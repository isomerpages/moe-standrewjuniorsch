<h3></h3>
<h1>School Transfer</h1>
<p>Thank you for your interest in joining the St Andrew's family.</p>
<p>Students who are currently enrolled in an MOE primary school and wish
to be considered for admission to St Andrew's School (Junior) may submit
a transfer application. Applications will only be considered if a vacancy
becomes available.</p>
<p><strong>Application Period</strong>
</p>
<p>• Applications close on <strong>1 August</strong> each year.</p>
<p>• Applications for the <strong>following year's waitlist</strong> will open
on <strong>1 October</strong> of the current year.</p>
<p><strong>Application Instructions</strong>
</p>
<p>• Submit your application using the online application form available <strong><a href="https://form.gov.sg/6a6fec1816c3471ba8aa2eb0" rel="noopener nofollow" target="_blank">HERE</a>.</strong>
</p>
<p>• Upload your child's <strong>latest school result slip</strong> via the
same application link.</p>
<p>• Only shortlisted applicants will be contacted.</p>
<p>• Shortlisted applicants may be required to sit for an internal assessment
and attend an interview as part of the selection process.</p>
<p>We appreciate your interest in St Andrew's School (Junior) and thank you
for your understanding that submission of an application does not guarantee
an offer of admission, as placement is subject to vacancies and the school's
selection process.</p>
